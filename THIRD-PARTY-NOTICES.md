# Third-party notices

Parallax is distributed under the MIT licence (see [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE)). It builds on and
redistributes work from the following projects, each under its own terms.

## shadcn-svelte

The registry-tier components under `src/lib/components/ui/` are generated by the
[shadcn-svelte](https://shadcn-svelte.com) CLI and follow its registry sources, themed through
design tokens rather than edits. shadcn-svelte is published under the MIT licence, as is the
[shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com) project it ports.

## Bits UI

The headless primitives most components compose. [Bits UI](https://bits-ui.com) is published
under the MIT licence.

## Lucide

All iconography is [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) via `@lucide/svelte`, published under the ISC
licence.

## Hanken Grotesk

The interface typeface, self-hosted via `@fontsource-variable/hanken-grotesk`. The font itself
is published under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the Fontsource packaging is MIT.

## Everything else

The remaining runtime and build dependencies (Svelte, Vite, Tailwind CSS, LayerChart,
d3-shape, and the rest) are declared in [`package.json`](./package.json) with their exact
versions locked in `package-lock.json`, and each retains its own licence. The production
bundle in `dist/` includes the runtime subset of these packages.
